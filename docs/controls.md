### Controls

| Action | Keyboard one | Keyboard two | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move / aim | WASD | Arrows | Left stick / D-pad |
| Jump / recovery | T | M | A |
| Charge / release bat | U | Period | X |
| Tongue / grapple | Y | Comma | B |
| Strafe | R | N | Left shoulder |
| Drop through platform | Down + Jump | Down + Jump | Down + A |

Hold **Strafe** to keep facing the same way while moving or using the tongue. Horizontal and diagonal tongue shots follow that facing; straight up/down aiming remains available, with downward shots requiring the frog to be airborne.

F11 -  fullscreen\
F3 - timing/network information\
F4 - collision geometry

Keyboard controls can be rebound in **Settings**. Escape is reserved for menus. Menu navigation is fixed: arrows or WASD, controller left stick or D-pad; Enter / controller A selects, Escape / controller B goes back. The game shows action buttons with DGR sprites.

Choose **Local** to enter the playable lobby. Space joins keyboard one, Right Shift joins keyboard two, and controller Start joins a controller. Use the tongue button to choose another available color, with the original game's spawn puff and sound; in team mode, up/down chooses a team. Press the same join button again to spawn in the assigned room and test your controls.

Escape opens the lobby pause menu. On a joined controller, Start opens it. **Start Match**, **Match Settings**, **Edit Slots**, personal settings and online options are listed vertically there. **Edit Slots** immediately selects rooms, with options and action hints inside the highlighted room. Selection stops at the edges and skips the center. Set the slot type to Open, Local or CPU, open/close it, or back out a local player/kick a remote player. At least two spawned players are needed to start.

Only the device that opened a lobby or pause menu can navigate it. Both keyboard layouts share the keyboard menu controls. Mouse clicks can activate menu actions when the keyboard owns the menu. Other players can keep moving or choosing colors in the lobby. Color prompts are hidden while a menu is open, but pending frogs and their effects remain visible. Use **Color / Team** to choose again, or **Back Out** to release your slot. **Return to Local Lobby** keeps local devices and color/team selections.

To open an online lobby, choose Steam or UDP/LAN. **Player Slots** goes straight to room selection. Open rooms determine total player capacity; the host can open or close rooms later in **Edit Slots**. Capacity and available slots are rebroadcast after edits. Joining ends when the host chooses **Start Match**. Steam invitations are available in the lobby pause menu.
