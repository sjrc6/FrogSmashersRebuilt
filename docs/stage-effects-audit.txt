Stage effects audit — 7 October 2026

Compared the seven original arena scenes, their referenced prefabs, stage
scripts, materials and particle modules with the rebuilt content and renderer.
Original source root: Originals/official/HohimBrueh/Assets/.

Result: one wholly missing stage system, with four linked behaviors, in the
Showdown bunker. All four are now restored. No other wholly missing stage
effect system was found in the other six scenes. The separate Finale fixes
from the New issues list are also included in this working tree.

Stage inventory

1BusStop
  Static scenery and Intro_Ambience_01 loop. Present. The original scene has
  no additional stage particle emitter or stage animation script.

2DownSmash
  Waterfall: six frames at 0.1 seconds; river: three at 0.15; fire: four at
  0.06; wheel: three at 0.175; water churn: six at 0.05. Present, with the
  original frame lists and Intro_Ambience_02 loop.

3Moon
  ShimmerWater reflection region and Ambience_Moon loop. Present.

4FinalFrogstination
  Static scenery and ambient loop. Present. The original scene has no
  additional stage particle emitter or stage animation script.

5Skyline
  Two-frame fire at 0.1 seconds, ShimmerSkyline heat distortion, far and near
  smoke emitters, ambient loop. Present. Smoke uses the original emission
  rates (100 and 75 per second), lifetimes (50 and 10 seconds), prewarm,
  colors, size growth, cone angles, velocities and noise strengths.
  Neither source smoke emitter has time-scrolling noise or lifetime fade.

6Finale
  Shimmer, animated fireball, seven parallax groups, escape ships with
  independently animated frog/flame layers, dropped background frogs,
  ambient flight loop and music. Present. The earlier New issues fixes
  restore ship layering and the muted frog palette, remove the decorative
  ghost fly, and stop stage music on victory while retaining ambience.

7Showdown (bunker tiebreaker)
  Elevator ambience was present. RandomLightFlicker had not been ported;
  its light was drawn at constant opacity and RoofDustParticle was an
  empty sprite-effect entry. Restored:
    - Light flashes, rapid flickering and decay to 0.2 opacity.
    - BackgroundExplosion clips at the original 0.5 source volume,
      multiplied by the sound group's existing volume and user volume.
    - Random camera impulses at each explosion, respecting Screen Shake.
    - Ceiling dust after strong explosions, on either side of the bunker.

Bunker source and tuning

  Scripts/Effects/RandomLightFlicker.cs
  Scenes/7Showdown.unity
  Prefabs/Effects/RoofDustParticle.prefab
  Sprites/Dust.mat and Sprites/white.png

  Explosions start immediately, then recur every 1–5 seconds. Intensity is
  0.3–1.3, with dust above 1.0. Ceiling positions use x = +/-9–21, y = 10.1.
  Each burst has 20–50 particles, size 0.1, lifetime 7.5 seconds, speed 7.5,
  a downward 10-degree cone of radius 0.1 and length 1.53, direction
  randomization 0.15, speed limit 2 with damping 0.15, and fading alpha.
  Noise strength/frequency are 0.5/0.5, with scrolling speed 1.
  The light shader preserves flash opacity above 1 instead of clipping it
  to the byte range used for sprite colors. Existing compiled textures and
  explosion clips are reused; the empty dust sprite-effect entry is removed.

Presentation behavior and verification

  Bunker timing and particles run on a cosmetic clock, outside simulation
  snapshots and rollback. Local pause freezes their clock and pauses sound.
  Score screens, scene exits and round changes clear stage presentation.
  Screen Shake only controls camera movement; light, dust and sound remain.

  Automated checks cover update cadence, explosion intervals and intensity,
  dust emission/expiry/fading, pause, bounded storage, rendered light and
  particle pixels, native audio playback, the shake toggle, rollback,
  round/scene changes and the actual FrogGame update path.

  Verification passed: Release build; 3,101 client checks with the audio
  backend; 115,128 core checks; all seven stage renders; bunker/Finale
  graphics and audio checks; 5,382 menu layout checks; match presentation
  flow checks; compiled-content hashes and asset references.

  This is a source/content audit plus rebuilt-engine verification, not a
  frame-by-frame comparison against a running Unity build. Particle noise
  and damping use the rebuilt emitter's approximation of Unity motion;
  the authored parameters and effect behaviors are retained.
